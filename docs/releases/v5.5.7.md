## Nano Banana Pro v5.5.7

### 日本語

- 配布ワークフローJSONを、添付された26ノード・30リンク・`PreviewImage`なしの標準20ステップ版へバイト単位で同期しました。
- STEP4とREADMEで、手動設定するComfyUI標準経路の `normal` と、配布専用版の15秒・16:9・0.4MP・`simple`・20ステップを区別しました。

### English

- Synced the distributed workflow JSON byte-for-byte with the supplied 26-node, 30-link, no-`PreviewImage` standard 20-step workflow.
- Distinguished the manually configured ComfyUI standard route's `normal` scheduler from the dedicated distribution's 15-second, 16:9, 0.4 MP, `simple`, 20-step defaults in STEP4 and the README.
- Clarified that the supported keys are `Google Gemini API` or `ComfyUI API` keys, that the local JSON credential store is unencrypted and outside the workflow JSON, and that custom-node loading can fail for additional reasons.
- Documented the post-H3 title and fixed-credit composition, including the single upper-left black title with a white outline and no background bar.
