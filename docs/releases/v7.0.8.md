# Nano Banana Pro v7.0.8

## English

- Improved narrative outfit continuity across panels and clarified that scripted clothing takes priority over reference-sheet uniforms. Explicit outfit changes, camera work, acting and panel art styles remain supported.
- Native 5–8-panel inspection now checks the identity disposition of every observed body. Unresolved identities remain unverified; ordinary background people are allowed. Vision models may still miss people, so visual review remains necessary.
- Existing native-mode images can be reinspected on OpenAI or Gemini without generating another image. Inspection API charges apply.
