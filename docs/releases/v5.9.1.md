### 日本語

### English

- Preserves prior failed local-only LFS commits in a local recovery ref before rebuilding the distribution from the current remote `main`; no published history is rewritten.
- Delivers ZIPs of 10 MiB or less through regular Git and preserves ZIP, workflow JSON, and SHA-256 sidecar bytes with `-text`; only future ZIPs over 10 MiB use LFS/Xet.
