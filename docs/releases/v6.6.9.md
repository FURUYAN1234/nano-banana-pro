# Nano Banana Pro v6.6.9

## 日本語

- 4コマのシナリオで、人物の位置・動作・視線と相手への反応をつなぎ、各コマの役割に応じたカメラ位置、仰俯角、距離、遠近を設計しやすくしました。
- 画像指示では、演技・表情・光・人物と背景の見分けやすさを保ちながら、衣装や小物のコマ間の連続性、後ろ向きの頭部・頭飾りの向き、話者と吹き出しの対応を明確化しました。
- 検査の不確かな所見だけで画像を再生成せず、進捗表示に自動再検査の理由と結果を示します。
- 生成開始時のスクロールと、完成画像の表示アニメーションを改善しました。

## English

- Four-panel scenarios better carry character positions, actions, eyelines and reactions across panels, and distinguish camera position, elevation, framing and perspective by panel role.
- Image instructions clarify cross-panel wardrobe and prop continuity, rear-head and accessory orientation, and speaker-to-balloon matching while preserving expressive acting, lighting, and character readability against backgrounds.
- Uncertain inspection findings alone no longer trigger image regeneration; progress logs explain automatic reinspection reasons and results.
- Improved scrolling when generation starts and the reveal animation for completed images.

## 検証 / Verification

- Full Node test suite, strict lint, pre-deploy checks and production build are run by the official release transaction.
- Visual QA remains evidence-based: unresolved observations are reported as unverified and are not presented as a guarantee of perfect image compliance.
