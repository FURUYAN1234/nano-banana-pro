# Nano Banana Pro v6.4.6

## 日本語

- 背景のポスター、看板、包装、メニュー、書籍表紙は、自然な図柄、アイコン、配色、枠、材質、レイアウトを保持します。
- 未指定文字を避けるために、印刷面全体を空白、灰色、ぼかし、ピクセル化、モザイク、検閲表示にする指示を廃止しました。
- 明示された物体文字は正確に保持し、未指定面では明確に読める語句、数字、URLの捏造を防ぎます。小さな文字要素は判読不能なデザイン記号として扱えます。
- v6.4.5で導入した、単独吹き出しを話者側へ置いて短いしっぽを指定話者へ結ぶ修正も含みます。複数吹き出しだけは従来どおり右から左の順序を維持します。

## English

- Background posters, signs, packages, menus and book covers retain natural artwork, pictograms, color blocks, borders, material and layout.
- The prompt no longer permits a whole printed surface to be blanked, greyed out, blurred, pixelated, mosaicked or censored merely to avoid incidental text.
- Explicit object text remains exact. Unspecified surfaces avoid invented clearly readable words, numbers and URLs; small typographic elements may remain unreadable design marks.
- This release also includes the v6.4.5 single-balloon fix: place a lone balloon on its mapped speaker's side and connect it with a short tail to that speaker, while retaining right-to-left ordering for multi-balloon panels.

## Verification

- Shared OpenAI and Gemini/Web prompt contract reproduced the old wording as a failing test and passed after the common-cause fix.
- Live validation used STEP2 `gpt-5.6-terra` and GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst/xhigh at 1024×1536. The generated page retained the expected title, dialogue, panel block, compact footer and v6.4.6 watermark; the user visually accepted the corrected background treatment.
