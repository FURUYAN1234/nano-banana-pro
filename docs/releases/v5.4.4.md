- STEP4 now has one MiniMax H3 / ComfyUI copy entry. Its single prompt includes publication title OCR, the opening title, and the ending credit.

- Japanese dialogue sent to H3 is verified kana-only to reduce kanji-reading errors. Every non-still shot receives one modest, purposeful camera move.
