# v6.8.3 — Application terms and distribution audit

## English

Unified application terms: ordinary personal, business and commissioned use, and monetizing users' own outputs remain free. Selling or redistributing the app for a fee, paid services exposing its functionality, or bundling it with paid information products requires prior written permission. Valid prior grants and third-party licenses remain available; no rights clearance for generated outputs is guaranteed. See LICENSE for the full scope.

Excluded the third-party PDF development skill from the source distribution and shared skill lock, with a pre-deploy regression gate against reintroduction. This does not change the application's PDF manuals or generation workflow. Existing v6.8.2 operation manuals remain applicable. Provider models, prices and generated-content behavior are unchanged.
